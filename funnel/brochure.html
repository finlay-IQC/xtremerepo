About Xtreme

A finish you notice.
For the right reasons.

Xtreme Commercial Services paints, decorates and plasters homes, developments and commercial buildings across Central, North and North West London. Our clients are homeowners, property developers and building owners who want the job done right first time.

After more than 10 years and over 100 projects, we know a finish is only as good as the preparation underneath it. So we fix the walls first, protect your home while we work, and tell you exactly what it will cost before we start.

How we run every job.

02

Prep done properly
